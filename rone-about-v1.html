About the house

Rone

A wardrobe for the space between formal and familiar.

Our approach

Less display. More distinction. Clothes that leave room for the person wearing them.

Rone is an independent fashion house shaped around lasting daily uniforms. We begin with proportion, touch and movement, then remove anything that does not earn its place.

The result is a wardrobe that can be worn often, combined instinctively and made more personal over time.

The house

Old codes, lived differently.

Rone takes the assurance of traditional dressing and loosens it for a new generation. The blazer is softer. The trouser has room. Leather goods are precise without feeling precious.

Nothing is reserved for an occasion. Every piece is intended to take part in a life.
