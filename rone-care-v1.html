Client care

Living with Rone.

Simple habits preserve hand, shape and surface over time.

01 / Knitwear

Rest and reshape.

Fold knitwear rather than hanging it. Air between wears, reshape while damp and store with natural moth protection.

02 / Tailoring

Give structure room.

Use a broad hanger, brush gently after wear and allow tailoring to rest before returning it to a garment cover.

03 / Leather

Keep the surface calm.

Avoid humidity, direct heat and overfilling. Remove light marks with a clean dry cloth and use a leather specialist for deeper care.

04 / Footwear

Rotate and recover.

Allow shoes to dry naturally, use cedar trees after wear and protect suede or leather with the correct specialist treatment.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Contact client care as soon as possible. We can usually adjust an order before preparation begins, but changes cannot be guaranteed once a piece has entered dispatch.

When will my card be charged?

Your payment method is authorized when the order is placed and charged when the order is prepared. Any temporary authorization normally clears according to your bank's timing.

How do I know whether a piece will be restocked?

Limited-run pieces are not always repeated. Save the piece or join the Rone list to receive an availability note if it returns.

Can underwear and swimwear be returned?

Yes, when unworn and returned with every hygiene seal intact. For health and safety, pieces with a removed or damaged seal cannot be accepted.

Do you offer repairs?

We assess repairs individually based on material, construction and component availability. Send photographs through the contact form under Product care or repair.

How do I update my delivery address?

Saved addresses can be changed in your Rone account. For an order already placed, contact client care immediately; carrier restrictions may apply after dispatch.
