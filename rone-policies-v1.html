Terms of sale.

These terms apply when you purchase a Rone piece through this website. Placing an order confirms your acceptance of the terms in effect at that time.

An order is an offer to purchase. We confirm acceptance when the order enters preparation. We may decline or cancel an order where a piece is unavailable, payment cannot be authorized, delivery information is incomplete or activity appears unauthorized.

Prices and payment

Prices are shown in US dollars unless another currency is selected. Applicable tax and delivery charges are presented before an order is placed. Your payment method may be authorized at checkout and charged when preparation begins.

Availability

Rone works in limited runs. A piece shown online may become unavailable before checkout is complete. If availability changes after purchase, we will contact you and reverse any related charge.

Delivery

Delivery dates are estimates and may change because of carrier, customs or destination conditions. Risk passes when the order is delivered to the address supplied at checkout. Review the full delivery information.

Returns and refunds

Eligible unworn pieces may be returned within 30 days with tags and protective materials intact. Hygiene seals must remain in place. Final-sale and personalized pieces are not returnable unless faulty.

We work to show colour, material and scale accurately. Displays vary, and natural materials may include small differences in tone or grain. These distinctions are part of the piece rather than faults.

Governing terms

How we use information

We use information to process purchases, deliver orders, prevent misuse, maintain accounts, respond to requests, improve our services and send communications you choose to receive.

Payment information

Payment details are processed by secure payment providers. Rone receives transaction status and limited card information, such as card type and final digits, rather than complete card credentials.

We keep information for as long as needed to provide services, meet legal and accounting duties, resolve disputes and prevent fraud. Retention periods vary by category and location.

Contact about privacy

Send a privacy request through client care. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request and will respond within the period required by applicable law.

Accessibility.

Rone is working toward an experience that can be used with a broad range of devices, preferences and assistive technologies.

Our approach

We aim to follow WCAG 2.2 Level AA guidance where practical and include accessibility in design, content, development and quality review.

Current provisions

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and controls.
  • Visible focus and descriptive form labels.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful imagery.
  • Responsive layouts and reduced-motion support.

Known limitations

Some third-party content and services may not yet offer the same level of access. Product photography and emerging prototype interactions continue to be reviewed as the site develops.

Feedback

If anything prevents you from shopping or finding information, contact us with the page, device and assistance needed. We will offer an alternative where possible and use the report to improve the experience.

Contact

Use the client-care form and choose Other, or email accessibility@rone.example. We aim to acknowledge accessibility feedback within two business days.
